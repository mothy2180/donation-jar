# docs/legal — documents to write before go-live (Milestone 5)

All user-facing legal text is **Bahasa Malaysia primary, English second** (PDPA
s.7(3); Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transactions) Regulations 2024
[P.U.(A) 449/2024]). Drafts live in the private ops repo
(`mothy2180/donation-jar-ops`) until legally reviewed; published pages ship as
Markdown under `apps/web/src/content/legal/{ms,en}/` so `scripts/copy-lint.sh`
scans them. This file is the internal checklist; it is exempt from copy-lint
because it has to discuss the banned wording.

## Draft wording (from the 2026-10-04/05 research; not yet legally reviewed — keep consistent until legal review)

Locale keys holding exempted text are listed in `scripts/copy-lint-allow.txt`.

**Badge for organisations approved under s.44(6)** — show the approval reference and validity only; never the HASiL/LHDN logo, and never a general claim that the organisation is approved by LHDN:
- BM: *Diluluskan di bawah subseksyen 44(6) Akta Cukai Pendapatan 1967 — rujukan LHDN.AG.600-12/1/4-6.XXXX, sah hingga hh/bb/tttt. Resit cukai rasmi dikeluarkan oleh [NGO], bukan oleh platform ini; potongan tertakluk kepada had 10% pendapatan agregat. Resit hanya kepada penderma yang memberikan nama penuh, nombor MyKad/pasport (atau nombor pendaftaran bagi syarikat dan organisasi) dan alamat penuh; tip dan tampungan caj tidak termasuk.*
- EN: *Approved under subsection 44(6) of the Income Tax Act 1967 — ref. LHDN.AG.600-12/1/4-6.XXXX, valid until dd/mm/yyyy. Official tax receipts are issued by [NGO], not by this platform; the deduction is capped at 10% of aggregate income. Receipts go only to donors who give their full name, MyKad/passport number (or registration number for companies and organisations) and full address; tips and fee covers are excluded.*

**Individual-case jars (paid to a registered payee; not deductible)**:
- BM: *Tabung ini mengumpul dana untuk [nama penerima manfaat]. Wang dibayar terus oleh penyedia pembayaran kepada [penerima bayaran berdaftar], sebuah [organisasi penaja / hospital / vendor] berdaftar yang wajib menggunakannya untuk [tujuan] dan melaporkannya di lejar awam — bukan kepada individu tersebut. [Nama penerima manfaat] tidak diluluskan di bawah subseksyen 44(6) Akta Cukai Pendapatan 1967, dan tiada resit cukai dikeluarkan bagi tabung ini walaupun [penerima bayaran berdaftar] diluluskan. Sumbangan kepada tabung ini TIDAK layak mendapat potongan cukai dan tiada resit pengecualian cukai akan dikeluarkan; anda hanya menerima pengesahan pembayaran.*
- EN: *This jar raises money for [beneficiary name]. The payment provider pays it directly to [registered payee], a registered [sponsoring organisation / hospital / vendor] that must apply it to [purpose] and account for it on the public ledger — not to the individual. [Beneficiary name] is not approved under subsection 44(6) of the Income Tax Act 1967, and no tax receipt is issued for this jar even if [registered payee] is approved. Contributions to this jar are NOT tax-deductible and no tax-exemption receipt will be issued; you will receive a payment acknowledgement only.*

**Tip paid to the platform (Model A jars only)**:
- BM: *Tip pilihan RM X dibayar kepada [Platform Sdn Bhd] untuk mengendalikan perkhidmatan ini; ia bukan derma kepada [penerima] dan tidak layak mendapat potongan cukai.*
- EN: *The optional RM X tip is paid to [Platform Sdn Bhd] to run this service; it is not a donation to [beneficiary] and is not tax-deductible.*

**Processing- and transfer-fee cover** (covers the processing fee of the method the donor chose plus Curlec's transfer fee, so on Model A jars the jar receives the full gift):
- BM: *Tampungan caj pilihan RM X dibayar kepada [Platform Sdn Bhd] untuk membayar caj pemprosesan dan caj pindahan Razorpay Curlec bagi derma ini supaya caj-caj itu tidak ditolak daripada sumbangan anda; ia bukan derma kepada [penerima] dan tidak layak mendapat potongan cukai.* On organisation jars add: *[Nama] menerima RM Z selepas caj platform RM P.*
- EN: *The optional RM X fee cover is paid to [Platform Sdn Bhd] to pay Razorpay Curlec's processing and transfer fees on this donation so they are not deducted from your gift; it is not a donation to [beneficiary] and is not tax-deductible.* On organisation jars add: *[Name] receives RM Z after the RM P platform fee.*

**Confirmation email**: must state *Ini bukan resit cukai / This is not a tax receipt.*

**Disallowed causes notice** (locale keys starting `disallowedCauses` and the Beneficiary Agreement's prohibited-collections clause are the only places these words may appear; each is listed in `scripts/copy-lint-allow.txt`):
- BM: *Kami tidak menerima kutipan zakat, fitrah, fidyah atau kaffarah — hanya amil yang dilantik oleh Majlis Agama Islam Negeri boleh mengutipnya. Kutipan wakaf tidak diterima buat masa ini. Tabung sedekah/masjid memerlukan kebenaran bertulis Majlis Agama Islam Negeri.*
- EN: *We do not accept zakat, fitrah, fidyah or kaffarah collections — only amil appointed by a State Islamic Religious Council may collect them. Wakaf collections are not accepted for now. Sedekah and mosque jars require the State Islamic Religious Council's written permission.*

**QR poster footer**: *Derma dalam talian sahaja — bukan untuk kutipan jalanan / Online donations only — not for street or house-to-house collection.*
