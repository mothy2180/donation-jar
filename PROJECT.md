## 1. Problem and promise

**Problem.** People who donate online in Malaysia cannot see (a) how much a fundraiser has *actually* received, in real time, and (b) what the money was spent on afterwards. Static DuitNow QR codes and e-wallet "Donate" buttons move money for free but give the donor no identity trail, no receipt and no spend trail — and impersonating a real NGO is the commonest donation fraud in Malaysia.

**Promise (as we may truthfully state it).**

1. *Confirmed, not claimed.* A donation appears in a jar's total only after the payment provider (Razorpay Curlec) has confirmed it **and** our server has re-checked the payment with Curlec. The public figure is labelled "confirmed by payment provider" — never "in the bank" — with separate *Transferred* and *Settled* badges.
2. *Live.* The jar page updates within seconds of confirmation.
3. *Spending must be itemised.* Jar owners must publish each outflow on a public ledger with evidence (receipt or e-invoice) within 30 days; unexplained balances are flagged in red. We cannot see the payee's bank account, so unreported spending shows up only as an unexplained balance, and self-declared items are labelled as such.
4. *Vetted.* No jar goes live before a human has reviewed the owner's documents, sanctions screening is clear, and the payment provider has created a payout account in the legal name of the registered payee — the organisation itself or, for an individual case, its sponsoring organisation or the hospital/vendor (never a personal account).
5. *Split at payment, never pooled.* Razorpay Curlec splits each donation at the moment of payment: the jar's share is transferred to the registered payee's own Curlec account and settled to its bank; only published fees, fee covers and tips stay with us. Donor money sits in our Curlec balance only in transit — the moment before the split, a failed transfer being retried (at most 24 h, then refunded) or a refund being processed — and each such amount is tracked per donation in our ledger and reported on the Trust page. We never pool donations or pay them out later.

**What we do not promise.** Automatic capture of every receipt (not possible in Malaysia today — see §7), reliable detection of AI-generated receipts, tax deductibility (only s.44(6)-approved bodies issue tax receipts, and only they can), or that a jar owner will spend wisely.

**Working name.** *Balang* (BM: jar). Product nouns: jar / balang, cause, beneficiary, verified organisation, transparency ledger. Words we never use in product copy: see §11.

The limited-purpose exemption (P.U.(A) 463/2024, in force 2 Jan 2025) covers only four categories — single-premise/single-brand purchases within RM1m liability/volume and RM500 wallet caps, rewards, refunds, telco prepaid — none of which fits a multi-jar donation wallet. | BNM Policy Document on Electronic Money, 31 Jan 2025 |

## 5. Data model and ledger

Conventions: all money `BIGINT *_sen`, `currency CHECK ('MYR')`; `uuid` ids; `created_at` everywhere; RLS enabled on every table; only schema `app` exposed; `ledger`, `psp`, `audit`, `ops` written solely by `SECURITY DEFINER` functions (pinned `search_path = ''`) called from Edge Functions over the direct database connection (`SUPABASE_DB_URL`), never through PostgREST. That connection carries no request JWT, so for staff actions the Edge Function verifies the caller's JWT (role in `app_metadata`, `aal = aal2`) and passes the actor id as a parameter; the role/`aal2` assertions live in the `app.*` RPCs that users call through PostgREST.

**State machines** in `app.state_transitions(entity, from_state, to_state, allowed_roles)`, enforced by one generic trigger that checks *who* and *what*:
- **jar**: draft → in_review → approved → live ⇄ paused → closed → archived; exits rejected, suspended. Trigger: live requires a confirmed `psp_linked_account_id` (two admins, or one admin after the 24 h cooling period while solo), a live verification case, clear screening, a verified `clbg_solicitation_approval` for any CLBG beneficiary or payee, `platform_settings.s446_onboarding_enabled` for any beneficiary or payee with an s.44(6) approval (`s446_approval_ref` set or found on lookup), and for `cause_type = islamic` a verified `religious_permissions` row covering the jar's cause, state and dates.
- **donation**: created → pending → confirmed → routed → settled; pending → failed | expired (**both re-enterable** — a Curlec order stays payable and a donor may retry a failed FPX); confirmed → route_failed; any → partially_refunded | refunded. Timestamps are the truth; `status` is derived; the public *Transferred* badge maps to status `routed`. There is no donor-pending ledger account: unconfirmed money can never enter totals.
- **verification case**: draft → submitted → screening → in_review ⇄ needs_more_info → approved_pending_psp → psp_requested → live; exits rejected, suspended.
- **expense**: draft → submitted → checking → in_review → approved → published | rejected | needs_info | withdrawn; published → reversed (new entry, never an edit).

**Core tables (`app`).** `profiles` (display name, locale; **no role column**), `beneficiaries` (kind: individual | sole_prop | company | clbg | society | ngo | trust | not_yet_registered; `legal_name_norm UNIQUE` = NGO name-claim lock; ## 6. Payment flow (per donation)

0. **Preconditions**: jar live (trigger-guaranteed confirmed Linked Account); active fee policy. Donate page shows presets RM20/50/100/200 (minimum RM10) and the live breakdown from `app.quote_donation` (gift, processing fee and Route transfer fee — each incl. service tax if Curlec confirms it — platform fee, unticked fee cover with the model-specific wording (§9), optional tip on Model A jars, total charged, "jar receives"). Receipt-eligible jars (valid s.44(6) reference) show an optional "Saya mahu resit cukai rasmi daripada [NGO] / I want an official tax receipt from [NGO]" step collecting the `donation_receipt_requests` fields with a consent naming the NGO as recipient; Islamic-cause jars show a separate unticked explicit-consent checkbox before Pay.
1. **Checkout** (`create-checkout`, `verify_jwt=false`): Turnstile → rate limits (10 checkouts per IP/device per 10 min; 50 per jar) → authoritative re-quote → `INSERT donations (created)` → Curlec Order `{ amount: gross_sen, currency: 'MYR', receipt: donation_id, payment_capture: 1, notes: { donation_id }, transfers: [{ account: psp_linked_account_id, amount: transfer_sen, currency: 'MYR', on_hold: false }] }` → `pending`. **Stop-ship**: production refuses to create orders if the adapter cannot attach order-level transfers; there is no capture-then-transfer fallback.
2. **PSP**: Curlec Standard Checkout redirect with `callback_url`. `checkout-callback` verifies `razorpay_signature = HMAC-SHA256(order_id|payment_id)` with constant-time compare and 303-redirects to `/d/:id?t=token`. It never credits anything. The status page says "Sedang diproses — kami hanya mengiranya selepas penyedia pembayaran mengesahkan / Processing — we only count it once the payment provider confirms it" and polls every 3 s.
3. **Webhook** (`psp-webhook-curlec`): raw body ≤1 MB → HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body vs **current and previous** secret (zero-downtime rotation) → `psp.ingest_event` (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`; event id = `x-razorpay-event-id`) → **200 within ~300 ms** → `EdgeRuntime.waitUntil(processEvent)`. Unknown kinds stored as `ignored`. Production IPs (43.216.167.81, 43.216.100.227, 43.216.239.250 — *secondary*: Curlec doc mirror, read 2026-10-04; confirm via day-1 email Q9; UAT 52.66.76.68, 52.66.95.207, 13.127.201.109) are checked inside the function against the client-IP header Supabase forwards (log and alert on mismatch until confirmed on staging) — our Cloudflare zone cannot see `*.supabase.co` traffic, so a WAF rule needs the Supabase custom-domain add-on (Rung 2). **Signature verification stays mandatory.**
4. **Truth-from-PSP**: resolve the donation by exact `(provider, psp_order_id)`; fetch `GET /v1/payments/{id}`, `/transfers`, `/refunds` and `GET /v1/orders/{id}/payments` (2.5 s timeouts; failure → retry); snapshot in `psp.objects`/`psp.payments`; compute the posting plan in TS; `ledger.apply_plan(donation_id, event_id, plan)` locks the donation `FOR UPDATE`, skips entry kinds that exist, inserts lines, updates `jar_balances`, `jar_feed`, projections and outbox in **one transaction**. Amount/currency/order mismatch → `quarantined` + alert, nothing posted. Returns applied | already_applied | retry | quarantined.
5. **Realtime**: `jar_feed` INSERT → broadcast → clients refetch; the three numbers and the "last confirmed donation Xs ago" ticker update in 1–2 s; the donor page flips to "Confirmed — acknowledgement emailed" (*not a tax receipt*).
6. **Cron** (pg_cron → pg_net → function with `x-cron-secret`; each function responds 202 immediately, claims a bounded batch with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED LIMIT 20`, works inside `waitUntil` with a 100 s budget, persists a cursor): every minute `process-events` + `send-email`; `sync-pending` with per-donation backoff 2/10/30/120 min then hourly to 24 h (`GET /v1/orders/{id}/payments` → synthesised `poll:` event through the same path); hourly webhook-silence alert; nightly 02:00 MYT `reconcile-psp` (diff Curlec's list endpoints vs DB; fetch facts only for mismatches; post settlements from `recipient_settlement_id`; `ledger.recompute_balances()`; `ops.reconciliation_runs`); weekly re-screening.
7. **route_failed SLA**: automatic `createTransfer` retries to the *same* confirmed Linked Account every 15 min for 6 h; if still unrouted before the next settlement cut-off (default 24 h) → auto-refund + reversing entries; such donations never show a *Transferred* or *Settled* badge; route-failed money is posted to `DONOR_FUNDS_HELD` from the failure until it is routed or refunded; any that reaches the platform's bank account raises an alert and is returned within 5 working days.
8. **Refunds** (admin, aal2; maker-checker above RM500): states refund_requested → reversal_pending → reversal_done → refund_pending → refunded. Pre-check `PLATFORM_FLOAT + PSP_CLEARING` (Curlec pays refunds from the merchant balance and keeps gateway fees — *secondary*: Curlec doc mirror; its terms of use say fees are payable even if refunded; confirm via day-1 email Q6); refuse when `JAR_ACCOUNTABLE` would go negative or a settled Linked Account cannot be reversed; `reverse_all=1` where supported; alert if a reversal completes without the refund within 24 h. Policy: platform error or duplicate within 7 days; otherwise the payee decides (the organisation, or for an individual case the sponsoring organisation or hospital/vendor, after consulting the beneficiary); FPX/e-wallet refunds take 0–3 days, cards 5–10; nothing older than 6 months. Dashboard-initiated refunds are applied by the mirror rule and flagged.

**Edge cases handled**: transfer.processed before payment.captured (the fetch shows both → one transaction); failed-then-paid order (`psp.payments` row per attempt; `failed → confirmed` allowed); first fact is `refunded` (capture + refund mirror posted together); uncaptured auto-refund (status failed, no lines); duplicate deliveries (inbox UNIQUE + keyed entries); webhook endpoint auto-disabled after 24 h of failures (silence alert + runbook; poller and recon heal from `GET /payments`); Supabase outage (Curlec retries 24 h; Pro = no pausing before real money).

## 7. Spend transparency

**Promise in the T&Cs and on every jar**: *"Jar owners must itemise every ringgit they spend from this jar on its public ledger, with evidence, within 30 days (per-jar configurable); balances left unexplained are flagged in red. We cannot see the payee's bank account, so unreported spending shows only as an unexplained balance, and self-declared items are labelled as such."* Not promised: automatic receipt capture; detection of AI-generated receipts (Anthropic's own docs say a model cannot determine that; we say so on the Trust page).

Why automatic capture is not possible in Malaysia (*verified 2026-10-04*): LHDN MyInvois has buyer-side APIs (client-credentials via "Register ERP"; intermediary login for organisations; `GET /documents/recent?InvoiceDirection=Received` limited to 31 days; `/documents/search`; `/documents/{uuid}/raw`; polling only) but suppliers below RM3 million revenue are exempt, consolidated e-invoices cover buyers who don't ask, and the ≤RM5m cohort may refuse individual e-invoices until 31 Dec 2027. Bank feeds: Finverse covers individual accounts at Maybank/CIMB/Public Bank/Hong Leong/RHB (US$0.50 per connected account), business accounts only at foreign banks; BNM's Open Finance exposure draft (ED 028-36, 18 Nov 2025; effective 1 Jan 2027 as drafted, final policy not yet issued) admits only licensed FSPs as data consumers. Gmail inbox access needs a paid CASA lab assessment annually. Platform-issued spend cards: Airwallex Malaysia card terms allow cards only for your own employees; Stripe Issuing and Wise Business are not in Malaysia — **dropped**.

**Flow.**
1. **Submit** (owner, aal2): amount, category (medical, education, food, shelter, logistics, equipment, admin ≤ published cap, other), description, merchant, date, optional MyInvois UUID, line items, receipt photo/PDF ≤10 MB. Browser resizes to ≤1600 px JPEG, reads EXIF capture time then strips all EXIF/GPS, uploads to `receipts/<jar>/<expense>/…`.
2. **Extract** (`receipt-extract`, every minute, ≤5 jobs): recompute SHA-256 and a 64-bit pHash **server-side in Deno** from the stored object (decode the ≤1600 px image; client values kept as tamper evidence; the expense stays in `checking` until `server_phash` is set); extractor `typed` (owner's fields, confidence 1.0) at beta; `llm` (`claude-haiku-4-5`, strict JSON schema, per-field confidence, ≈US$0.004/receipt) behind a flag with a consent line naming Anthropic as a processor — medical categories excluded. Extraction never auto-approves.
3. **Checks** (`app.run_expense_checks`): line items sum to total (±5 sen); date within jar period; EXIF vs claim ≤3 days; duplicate sha256/pHash (Hamming ≤10) across **all** jars; fingerprint uniqueness; velocity (>5 expenses or >30% of available in 24 h → warn); overspend → fail; admin-share cap; MyInvois UUID format (Phase 2: validated via `/documents/{uuid}/raw`).
4. **Review** (admin origin): queue sorted by jar risk; typed vs extracted side by side; image via 60 s signed URL; approve → `expense_published` entry, `evidence_tier` (2 reviewed receipt, 3 self-declared), public line, audit row; >RM5,000 needs a distinct second approver (24 h cooling while the founder is alone).
5. **Publish**: public two-sided timeline (money in | running balance | money out with badges *Receipt reviewed* — tooltip "A reviewer checked this receipt against the claim; we cannot detect every forged or AI-generated receipt." — / *Self-declared* / *Flagged*); images private (owner may publish a manually redacted copy); jar health card (days since update, % spend with reviewed receipts, verification tier); **unexplained balance** rule: Available untouched for `accountability_days` after `ends_at` or 90 days of inactivity → red; FIFO "where did my ringgit go" for logged-in donors.
6. **Flag**: any visitor (Turnstile, 3/hour/IP); 3 unresolved flags mark the expense flagged publicly and pause new publications for that jar.
7. **Keyword screen** (BM/EN) on titles, purposes, updates and expense descriptions: zakat/fitrah/fidyah/kaffarah → blocked with the appointed-amil explanation; wakaf → blocked pending legal advice; sedekah/masjid/surau/tahfiz/qurban/infaq → forces `cause_type = islamic` (religious-permission gate); only a reviewer can reclassify after checking the purpose — rewording does not clear the flag; the same screen holds for review any owner-written text matching copy-lint's banned list (social exchange / social impact project, LHDN/HASiL-approved, tax-deductible / potongan cukai / pelepasan cukai, investment/returns, wallet/top-up) — tax-status wording appears only in the platform's own badge; the religious list includes the spellings fidiah and kafarah; every title/purpose edit after go-live is re-screened; flagged updates held for review.
8. **Retention**: evidence files and extraction JSON 7 years (`retain_until`, `legal_hold`); public ledger amounts and expense lines kept indefinitely (append-only, ids and amounts only); donor display names and messages shown while the jar is live and for 12 months after closure, then replaced by "Penderma / Donor" in `public_ledger_lines` (immediately on a withdrawal-of-consent request); an individual-case jar's beneficiary name, photos and medical details removed from public pages 90 days after closure; this schedule is stated in the privacy notice. Third-party health data (hospital letters, diagnoses) is sensitive: `subject_consent` document mandatory when fundraising for someone else; medical evidence never public without manual redaction.

Phase 2 (legal gating where custody re-appears): per-jar inbound receipt email (Cloudflare Email Routing → Email Worker, `postal-mime`, HMAC-signed POST), MyInvois received-documents polling for organisation beneficiaries, platform-paid vendor invoices only via beneficiary-owned payout accounts with written legal advice.

## 8. Onboarding, KYC and data custody

**Not self-serve by design.** Curlec Support creates Linked Accounts (24-hour cooling period, MYR only), so the queue has explicit states `approved_pending_psp → psp_requested → live`; the owner UI says honestly that payout-account creation takes days to weeks.

**Documents.**
- *Organisation (launch cohort)*: SSM/ROS/ROC certificate; for a CLBG (company limited by guarantee — identify it from the company type on the SSM e-Info profile, not from "Berhad", which licensed CLBGs may omit and public share companies also use), the SSM Registrar's prior approval to solicit donations from the public (para 23(d)) — missing approval = reject; constitution / Form 9 equivalent; authorised-signatory letter; **signatory identity verified through the KYC vendor** (not uploaded to us); bank-statement header in the organisation's name (transactions blacked out); s.44(6) approval letter if approved (unlocks the approval-reference badge; expiry tracked; such organisations wait for the SC answer, N4; "Not tax-deductible" everywhere else); Islamic causes: the State Islamic Religious Council's written permission, recorded per jar.
- *Individual cases (behind the `individuals_enabled` flag)*: the person in need is the **beneficiary**, but the **payee** (the Curlec Linked Account) is always a registered entity — a sponsoring society whose registered objects permit helping unrelated individuals (*unconfirmed* — confirm with ROS/legal), a CLBG holding SSM's approval to collect from the public, or a Sdn Bhd, that accepts the sponsor variant of the Beneficiary Agreement for that case, or the hospital/vendor that will be paid (preferred for medical jars). Direct payouts to a personal account stay off (`direct_individual_payouts = false`, see N12 and ADR 0010). Controls: identity + liveness of the patient or guardian via the KYC vendor (verdict only); medical certificate or other cause evidence; **subject consent** when the money is for someone else; per-jar cap RM50,000 and ≤90 days; one active individual-case jar per MyKad; payee name must equal the registered payee's legal name.

**Custody (ADR 0005).** Identity images and biometric outputs live with the KYC vendor (Didit: EU hosting, MyKad/MyKid/MyPR/passport support, retention set to 30 days, template retention off; free tier 500 checks/month until 31 Oct 2026, then US$10 credit/month ≈ 30 checks, list price ≈ US$0.33 per full check — *verified 2026-10-04*). Curlec holds its own Linked-Account KYC under its merchant due-diligence duties (legal basis and retention period *unconfirmed* — day-1 email Q13). We keep: (1) a **7-year decision record** — legal name, `ic_hmac`, masked bank account + encrypted number, registration numbers, screening results, vendor session id + verdict, decision, reviewers, Linked Account id, document SHA-256 hashes; (2) documents in the private `kyc-docs/` bucket in `ap-southeast-1` on **one schedule** (identical in ADR 0005), set as `verification_documents.retain_until` per doc_type: bank-statement header, signatory letter and cause evidence (e.g. medical certificates) — purged **90 days after the decision** (30 for rejections); SSM/ROS/ROC certificate, CLBG solicitation approval and constitution — 7 years after the relationship ends; s.44(6) letter and religious permission — life of the jars they authorise + 7 years; `subject_consent` — while any personal data of the subject is processed or published + 7 years (consent metadata also in `audit.log`). KYC-related Edge Functions pinned to `ap-southeast-1` (`x-region`). The `kyc-docs` bucket is never backed up to Backblaze/R2 (R2 has no Asia jurisdiction) — the decision record is what must survive. Phase-2 option if we ever hold identity images: browser-side envelope encryption with owner-held keys, or Malaysia-resident storage (AWS ap-southeast-5 S3 ≈ US$0.0225/GB-month; RDS db.t4g.micro ≈ US$17/month).

**Cross-border legal basis.** Documented Transfer Impact Assessment for each recipient (Supabase Singapore; Didit EU; Cloudflare; Resend; Sentry; Curlec/Razorpay incl. India processing; Backblaze/R2 for non-KYC backups; GitHub; Anthropic if enabled), valid ≤3 years, plus recorded consent via the bilingual notice with a **separate explicit-consent checkbox** for sensitive data (identity verification, religious-cause jars, third-party health data). DPIA before go-live (facial recognition is a named trigger). Data-subject request runbook. DPO appointment alert at 8,000 distinct data subjects of any kind (donors + owners + signatories + beneficiaries/patients + guardians; all donors counted as financial-data subjects) — thresholds 20,000 / 10,000; appoint and register within 21 days.

**Screening.** Weekly GitHub Action (Python) parses the official UN consolidated list XML and MOHA s.66B list into `app.sanctions_entries` (OpenSanctions mirrors are CC BY-NC — not used). `app.screen_case`: `pg_trgm` similarity ≥0.6 on normalised names + exact IC-HMAC/registration/DOB matches → `screening_hits`; any hit blocks approval until a reviewer records a disposition; live beneficiaries re-screened weekly; a true positive suspends the beneficiary, pauses its jars and asks Curlec to hold transfers.

**Review (admin origin, aal2).** Checklist per document type; every organisation applicant and payee looked up on LHDN's Semakan Kelulusan Derma — an s.44(6)-approved one is held at `needs_more_info` until the SC's written answer is filed and `s446_onboarding_enabled` is set, whether or not it uploaded its letter; duplicate-applicant alert on `ic_hmac`; name-claim lock; **mule-account control: bank holder name must equal the verified legal name — mismatch is an automatic reject**; SSM e-Info profile (RM15.40) bought only after preliminary approval; decision emails bilingual. **Maker-checker**: `require_two_approvers` on the day a second admin exists; until then the single admin confirms only after a mandatory 24-hour cooling period, and the named second owner reviews the audit log weekly — stated honestly on the Trust page.

**Linked Account.** On `approved_pending_psp` the admin generates the Curlec Support request pack (legal name, business_type, bank, holder, account number decrypted on demand, contact) via `adapter.createLinkedAccount()` (returns the manual instructions and email template); KYC material goes to Curlec only via its secure upload or encrypted attachments, logged in `audit.log`; when Curlec returns `acc_…`, admin A enters it (`verified_by`); admin B — or, while solo, the same admin after the 24-hour cooling period — confirms the name/bank match (`confirmed_by`) → case live → jar may go live.

**Bank changes.** New `bank_change` case: step-up TOTP (≤5 min), email + in-app notice, `hold_until = now() + 72 h` (old account keeps receiving; owner may pause), then the same Support update and the same confirmation rule as a new Linked Account (a second admin, or while solo the same admin after the 24-hour cooling period).

## 9. Fee engine and revenue model

**Engine.** `app.fee_policies(scope global|jar, applies_to, effective_from/to, rules jsonb)` — most specific effective row wins; history kept forever and listed on `/fees`. One STABLE `SECURITY DEFINER` function `app.quote_donation(jar, gift_sen, method, cover, tip_sen)` callable by anon returns `(policy_id, gift, platform_fee, est_processing, est_transfer_fee, cover_fees, beneficiary_processing, tip, gross, transfer, platform_net_est, lines jsonb)`; the UI renders the result (no second implementation to drift); `create-checkout` re-runs it, freezes `fee_quote`, and the Curlec Order uses `gross_sen` with `transfers[0].amount = transfer_sen` — the split shown is the split executed. Method is locked before Checkout.

`rules` keys: `min_donation_sen` 1000 · `suggested_sen` 2000 · `presets_sen` · `max_sen` by method · `platform_fee { side: none|beneficiary, bps ≤ 1000 (hard CHECK), cap_sen }` · `cover_fees { enabled, default_on: false }` — the cover equals the estimated processing fee on the gross plus Curlec's transfer fee, each incl. service tax, so a covered Model A gift transfers in full · `uncovered_processing_bearer: beneficiary|platform` (default beneficiary — the platform never silently subsidises) · `tip { enabled, mode: opt_in, presets_sen }` · `processor.curlec.{fpx: 150 bps min 100 sen, duitnow: 120 bps min 30 sen, tng/boost/grabpay: 150 bps, card: 240 bps}` · `route_transfer_bps` 25 (*unconfirmed* — Curlec's docs example; actual rate per plan) · `fee_tax_bps` 800 (SST on PSP fees — *unconfirmed*: Curlec's page says "18% GST", an India template; confirm in writing).

**Launch configuration (Model C, founder decision 2026-10-04).**
- Organisations (beneficiary kind company | clbg | society | ngo | trust): **Model B** — platform fee `side = beneficiary`, starting point 300–500 bps with a per-donation cap; processing and transfer fees itemised; cover-fees offered **unticked** with the Model B wording ("Tambah RM X untuk menampung caj pemprosesan dan caj pindahan pembayaran — [Nama] menerima RM Z selepas caj platform RM P / Add RM X to cover payment-processing and transfer fees — [name] receives RM Z after the RM P platform fee"; never "the full RM Y"); no tip prompt in v1. Public line: *"RM50 gift · fees −RM3.71"* (platform fee RM2.50 + processing RM1.08 + transfer fee RM0.13).

- Both: minimum RM10, RM20 suggested; gifts under RM20 steered to DuitNow Pay (once Curlec grants early access) or wallets; the all-in total is shown before the pay button.

## 10. Security checklist (ASVS 5.0 Level 2 target; OWASP Top 10:2025)

Do first (MVP):
1. **Checkout**: hosted/redirect only (Curlec Standard Checkout; FPX is a bank redirect) — card data never touches our code (SAQ A eligibility for Curlec Standard Checkout is *unverified*: obtain Curlec's PCI DSS attestation of compliance before enabling cards; SAQ A r1 also requires confirming the site is not susceptible to script attacks).
2. **Webhook**: HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body with constant-time compare against current **and previous** secret; 1 MB cap; 401 before any write; unique event id in the append-only inbox; 200 fast; nothing credited from a payload — only from a server-side re-fetch; quarantine on mismatch; poller + nightly reconciliation heal lost webhooks; Curlec IP check inside the function as defence in depth.
3. **Ledger**: double-entry BIGINT sen; deferred balance trigger; append-only grants + RAISE triggers; keyed entry kinds; `FOR UPDATE` per donation; role-aware state-transition trigger; nightly recompute and reconciliation with alerts.
4. **RLS**: on every table and `storage.objects`; only schema `app` exposed; projection tables for public reads; `SECURITY DEFINER` functions pin `search_path = ''` and assert role + `aal2`; pgTAP denial sweep; anon cannot execute `ledger.*`/`psp.*` or any `app.*` wrapper; Security Advisor zero ERROR before every release; roles in `app_metadata` only.
5. **Auth**: NIST 800-63B-4 passwords (≥15 chars, no composition rules, blocklist); TOTP for owners/reviewers/admins; Turnstile; 1 h JWT with refresh rotation; leaked-password protection with Pro; step-up for bank/fee/refund actions.
6. **Payout / account takeover**: Linked Account holder = verified legal name; two-admin confirmation (24 h cooling until then); bank change = step-up + notice + 72 h hold; maker-checker above RM5,000 (expenses) / RM500 (refunds); new-beneficiary jar cap; minimum donation; velocity limits per IP/device/jar; payouts only to registered payees; staff never hold, view or operate a beneficiary's bank login, card, OTP device or Curlec dashboard (Penal Code ss.424A–C).
7. **Web**: allowlist CSP without `unsafe-inline` (self + Curlec checkout host + Turnstile; `connect-src` Supabase; `frame-ancestors 'none'`); HSTS preload; nosniff; Referrer-Policy; Permissions-Policy; bearer tokens only (no cookie/CSRF surface); no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`; admin on a separate origin behind Cloudflare Access.
8. **Audit**: append-only `audit.log` via triggers + explicit rows for KYC access, PSP forwards, refunds, adjustments; 7 years.
9. **Backups / DR**: nightly `supabase db dump` (roles, schema, `--data-only --use-copy` separately) `| age` → Backblaze B2 (30 daily + 12 monthly), R2 after go-live; **the `kyc-docs` bucket is excluded**; monthly restore drill on the founder's machine in Malaysia with `ledger.check_invariants()`; Supabase Pro daily backups as the second copy; RPO 24 h / RTO 4 h; the `age` private key and `EMAIL_ENC_KEY` never enter GitHub.
10. **Pipeline + response**: Dependabot; Semgrep CE; `aquasecurity/trivy-action` ≥ v0.35.0 (its README recommends v0.36.0) and `setup-trivy` newer than v0.2.6, each pinned to a full commit SHA — never the Trivy binary or image v0.69.4–v0.69.6 (the Trivy ecosystem was compromised 19–23 Mar 2026; current release v0.75.0, 1 Oct 2026); verify downloads with cosign; gitleaks pre-commit and in CI over full history + GitHub push protection + private vulnerability reporting enabled; OpenSSF Scorecard; all actions pinned to commit SHAs; SBOM per release; `/.well-known/security.txt`; one-page incident response plan with PDPA timelines; quarterly key-rotation runbook (webhook secret dual-accepted during rotation); MFA + two owners on Supabase, GitHub and Cloudflare.

Also: PII minimisation (donor email encrypted with a blind index; hashed IP/device with 90-day retention; PSP payload PII redacted at ingest; Sentry scrubbing); `platform_settings.checkout_enabled` kill switch; health endpoint 503 on staleness so UptimeRobot alerts out-of-band; outbox priority classes (alert > acknowledgement > notification) with headroom for auth mail under Resend's 100/day.
